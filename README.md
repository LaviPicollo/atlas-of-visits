# Atlas of Visits

Colour in the countries, provinces and cities you have visited.

Three maps — countries, first-level provinces and states, and towns and cities
over about 15,000 people. Search or tap to mark a place; it colours in and joins
the list below the map. Pinch, drag and double-tap to move around.

Installs as an app: open the site in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home
Screen**. It then opens full screen with its own icon and works with no
connection, because the maps are cached on the device.

Your places are stored on the device. **Back up my places** writes a small JSON
file you can keep or send to another device, and **Restore from a backup**
reads it back.
